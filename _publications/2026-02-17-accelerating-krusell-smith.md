This paper substantially accelerates the Krusell-Smith method, particularly the simulation stage in models with nontrivial market-clearing conditions. By precomputing agents' optimal responses to different prices, the method eliminates the need to repeatedly solve their decision problems during simulation while preserving the standard Krusell-Smith structure. In the Bloom et al. (2018) model, the proposed grid-based method reduces simulation time from 290 minutes to 1.5 minutes and total computation time from approximately four days to 108 minutes, while preserving benchmark accuracy. To accommodate larger models, I extend the approach using neural networks with a discrete-continuous policy representation that preserves exact inaction and the discontinuous adjustment rules generated by fixed adjustment costs.

I then use the neural-network method to revisit aggregate comovement after a pure uncertainty shock in the Bloom et al. (2018) model. While the original specification fails to generate simultaneous declines in output, consumption, investment, and employment on impact, making the Frisch elasticity finite is sufficient to generate the missing comovement, without an accompanying adverse first-moment shock.
